# Opening instruction PowerPoint outline

The live instruction deck should be short enough to brief participants immediately before the 75-minute simulation without turning the exercise into a lecture.

## Slide 1 — Title
2027 Endymion Severe Weather DSS Exercise

## Slide 2 — Scenario
- Saturday, February 6, 2027
- Krewe of Endymion
- Scheduled 4:00 PM start
- Samedi Gras / pre-parade crowd already active
- New Orleans Mardi Gras Unified Command is the simulated partner

## Slide 3 — Team structure
Three-person teams:
- Radar Operator
- Mesoanalyst
- DSS Meteorologist

Facilitators float between teams and operate exercise control.

## Slide 4 — How information should flow
- Radar Operator tracks storm-scale evolution
- Mesoanalyst maintains environmental/lightning/MRMS context
- DSS translates that information into partner-facing recommendations
- Partner questions should drive targeted requests back to Radar/Meso

## Slide 5 — Systems you will use
- LIX SimOps — live DRT, radar/meso, partner injects
- Google Classroom — briefing/resources/links/debrief
- Mock Shift Log — coworker-owned Google Form feeding a Google Sheet

## Slide 6 — Google Classroom
Explain where briefing materials, reference resources, SimOps link, shift-log link, and debrief question live.

## Slide 7 — Mock Shift Log
DSS logs every meaningful external communication to/from Event Command.

## Slide 8 — Exercise timing
- Approximately 75 minutes including pauses/decisions
- Simulation window: 2:45–4:30 PM CST
- Default playback: 1.5x
- Facilitators may pause/change speed
- Partner messages release automatically by simulation time

## Slide 9 — Lightning decision framework
- 15 mi — elevated monitoring/preparation
- 12 mi — impending threat
- 8 mi — hold/suspend threatened operations
- Organized convection — account for 30+ minutes lead time

## Slide 10 — Other decision triggers
- Severe/Tornado Watch
- Severe/Tornado Warning
- Damaging winds
- Flash flooding / route-access impacts

## Slide 11 — Ground rules
- Treat the case as live
- Do not search for or identify the historical source case
- Do not look ahead
- Stay in assigned role
- Communicate verbally within the team
- DSS owns partner communication and the shift log
- Facilitators will float and troubleshoot but will not coach toward an answer

## Slide 12 — Start checklist
- Classroom open
- SimOps open and connected
- Correct team/role selected
- Shift Log open for DSS
- Required tools/resources open
- Wait for exercise control to start the clock
