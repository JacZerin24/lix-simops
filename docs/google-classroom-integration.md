# Google Classroom integration

Google Classroom should support the exercise, but **LIX SimOps remains the authoritative live simulation system**.

The reason is timing: Classroom announcements and materials can be scheduled by wall-clock date/time, but the exercise may pause, accelerate, or slow the displaced-real-time clock. Operational partner injects therefore belong in LIX SimOps, where they are keyed to simulation time.

## Recommended Classroom structure

### 00 — Start Here
Publish before the workshop.

- Exercise title and date
- Brief description
- What participants need open
- Link to LIX SimOps
- Join/setup instructions
- Link to the instruction PowerPoint
- Reminder not to search for or identify the historical source case

### 01 — Exercise Briefing
Publish at the beginning of the exercise.

- Endymion scenario brief
- Role assignments
- Team assignments
- Parade route / event context
- Exercise weather thresholds quick reference
- Ground rules

### 02 — Operational Reference Resources
Publish before the exercise or at the briefing.

- Lightning 15/12/8-mile weather thresholds; partner retains start/hold/resumption decisions
- NWS event-ready/lightning references
- Any local IDSS procedures that participants are expected to use
- Danielle's resource links, if appropriate
- Tool/reference cheat sheets

### 03 — Participant Tools
Publish before start.

- LIX SimOps link
- Coworker-owned mock shift-log Google Form link
- Any approved archived/web resources that participants are expected to use
- Technical troubleshooting notes

### 04 — Debrief
Keep unpublished until the exercise is complete or release manually.

- Short-answer reflection/question
- What weather information changed your assessment or informed the partner's decision?
- Which product/tool added the most value?
- Where did team communication break down?
- What should LIX standardize for future on-site/remote DSS?
- Link to any post-exercise survey

## Stream use

Use the Stream for:

- Start-of-exercise announcement
- Technical notices
- Non-scenario administrative updates
- Debrief availability

Do **not** use Classroom's wall-clock scheduled announcements as the primary severe-weather/partner inject system. Google notes that scheduled announcements can post later than the exact requested time, and a paused/accelerated simulation would cause them to drift from the meteorology.

## Suggested participant workflow

1. Open Classroom first.
2. Open the instruction PowerPoint and briefing materials.
3. Open LIX SimOps in a separate tab/window.
4. Join the assigned team and role.
5. DSS Meteorologists also open the mock shift-log Google Form.
6. Once the facilitator starts the exercise, treat LIX SimOps as the source of truth for simulation time and partner messages.
7. Return to Classroom after the exercise for the debrief/reflection.

## Classroom capabilities used

Current Google Classroom supports:

- Topics to organize classwork/materials
- Materials with Drive files or links
- Announcements
- Scheduled announcements/materials
- Short-answer and multiple-choice questions
- Google Forms attachments/quiz assignments
