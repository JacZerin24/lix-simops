**Do not distribute this file to participants.** It reveals the historical case.

The Endymion exercise uses the May 12, 2021 New Orleans tornado as its hidden
meteorological source. Historical local time is shifted forward **13 hours
45 minutes** before being mapped to February 6, 2027.

## Primary historical anchor

The EF0 tornado began around 2:05 AM CDT, tracked about 5.26 miles through
New Orleans, and lifted around 2:12 AM. Estimated peak wind was 85 mph.

## Facilitation principle

Do not force teams to recreate historical warning decisions. The source case
supplies realistic meteorology; the exercise evaluates **DSS reasoning, lead
time, communication, and team coordination** for the Endymion scenario.
