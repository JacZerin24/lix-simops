# Mardi Gras DSS scenario

This is the first planned end-to-end LIX SimOps scenario.

## Exercise design

Teams of three:

1. Radar Operator
2. Mesoanalyst
3. DSS Meteorologist

Facilitators remain unassigned to teams and float between groups while operating the simulation.

## Status

The dates, venue, archived meteorological case, event thresholds, partner injects, and datasets are placeholders until the case is selected.

Do not treat the sample times in the YAML files as operational dates.
