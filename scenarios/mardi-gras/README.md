# Mardi Gras DSS scenario

This scenario is the operational Endymion displaced-real-time exercise for LIX SimOps.

## Exercise design

Teams of three:

1. Radar Operator
2. Mesoanalyst
3. DSS Meteorologist

Facilitators remain unassigned to teams and float between groups while operating the simulation.

## Live exercise

- Exercise date: February 6, 2027
- Simulation window: 2:45–4:30 PM CST
- Scheduled parade start: 4:00 PM CST
- Default playback: 1.5×
- Primary partner construct: Mardi Gras Unified Command / parade operations

The participant package is case-blind. Historical source-case identity and timing belong only
in facilitator-controlled material such as `source-case.yaml` and the source-case timeline.

## Product status

The built case includes KLIX Level II radar, RAP environment, GOES-16 infrared, observed GLM,
MRMS products, and surface observations. LightningCast is optional until archived ABI inputs
are processed externally through CSPP Geo and staged into the case.

Do not expose the historical source case or facilitator timeline to participants.
